# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want this project to be a welcoming and respectful space for everyone,
regardless of their background, identity, or level of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate in discussions and reviews.
- Provide constructive feedback and accept it in good faith.
- Respect differing perspectives and experiences.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and help keep the community welcoming.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional community.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to
[sametakbal7@gmail.com](mailto:sametakbal7@gmail.com). You may also contact
the maintainer through [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/sametakbal/).
Do not post sensitive details in public issues.

The maintainer will review reports on a best-effort basis and handle them
as confidentially as possible. Depending on the situation, the maintainer
may remove content, request changes, or restrict participation in the project.
Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith is not acceptable.

This code applies to project spaces and when representing the project in
public spaces.
